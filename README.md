## Featured Projects

### [Premier League Odds Calibration](https://github.com/Joshc386/premier-league-odds-analysis) — Python

- **Status:** Complete
- **Summary:** Tests whether Bet365's Premier League 1X2 closing odds are well calibrated across 24 seasons and 9,120 fixtures. Overall the prices are almost exactly calibrated, but splitting the results by outcome shows that home wins are slightly under-priced and that draws in March are a statistically significant anomaly.

### [Sales Analysis](https://github.com/Joshc386/Sales-Analysis-Project) — Excel

- **Status:** Complete
- **Summary:** Analyses a Kaggle sales dataset to find insights that could guide business decisions. It covers revenue, profit and margin trends by month and year, and each sales rep's performance. The data is cleaned in Power Query and analysed with PivotTables and lookup formulas.

### [Assessment of Energy Forecasting Accuracy](https://github.com/Joshc386/Assessment-of-Energy-Forecasting-Accuracy) — Excel

- **Status:** In progress
- **Summary:** Measures how accurate NESO's day-ahead forecasts of UK electricity demand are by comparing them with the demand that actually occurred.

## AI-Assisted Builds

These projects were built with Claude, an AI coding assistant. I set the goals, made the design decisions and checked the results, and the AI wrote most of the code. I include them to show how I work with AI on larger systems. They are separate from the projects above because those show my own hands-on work.

- **[pl-betting-system](https://github.com/Joshc386/pl-betting-system):** A statistical betting system for Premier League and Championship goals markets. It combines several models, removes the bookmaker margin from the odds, sizes stakes with the Kelly criterion and tracks its own performance.
- **[wc-bet-system](https://github.com/Joshc386/wc-bet-system):** A Monte Carlo simulator for the 2026 World Cup, using Elo ratings and a Dixon-Coles model. It was backtested on nine past tournaments.
- **[BetStatsInterface](https://github.com/Joshc386/BetStatsInterface):** A football statistics platform. It collects data on a schedule into PostgreSQL and shows it through a FastAPI back end and a React front end.
- **[polymarket-momentum-sniper](https://github.com/Joshc386/polymarket-momentum-sniper):** A paper-trading bot for Polymarket's 5-minute Bitcoin markets. It has a risk-management layer, a kill switch, and backtests that test its results against unseen data.
